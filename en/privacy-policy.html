Evde Fitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy applies to the iOS app Evde Fitness (Home Workout for Men), developed by Hasan Berke Çakmak. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is protected.

Data we collect

The information you enter during onboarding (goal, height, weight, year of birth, injuries and similar), your workout history and your weight log are never sent to any server; they are stored only on your device. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase information described under "Purchases and subscriptions" below.

Third parties, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking. The only third-party service we use is RevenueCat, the subscription infrastructure that validates purchases (see below). Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

Purchases and subscriptions

Evde Fitness Premium purchases and subscriptions are made through Apple's App Store; Apple processes all payments. Your card number, billing address and other payment details never reach the app or us.

Your purchases are validated through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription infrastructure provider. For this, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID randomly generated by the app, the purchase and transaction information provided by Apple (such as the plan purchased, purchase and renewal dates, trial period, price and currency, and the App Store transaction ID), and the technical information needed to process the request (such as app and iOS version, language and App Store country). This information is used only to validate purchases, unlock Premium, manage your subscription (renewals, cancellations, refunds, restores) and give us aggregated sales statistics. Your name, email address, payment details and the health and workout information you enter in the app are never sent to RevenueCat; the anonymous ID does not identify you personally and is not used for tracking or advertising. How RevenueCat processes data is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

The app also stores a "Premium active" flag on your device so it can open quickly. How Apple processes purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Workout reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all data permanently deletes all of your data on the device. Deleting the app also removes all data on the device. These actions do not delete the anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat or Apple's purchase records; if you would like the RevenueCat record deleted, write to the address below (you may need to include the order ID from your App Store receipt so we can find it).

Children

Evde Fitness is not directed at children under 13.
