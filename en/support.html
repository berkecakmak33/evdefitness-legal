Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Your profile, plan, workout and weight history are stored only on your device. Premium purchases are validated anonymously through RevenueCat (details: Privacy Policy).

How do I change my plan?
Change your goal, number of days or workout length in Settings → Profile and your plan is rebuilt.

I don't get reminders.
Turn on notifications in iOS Settings → Evde Fitness → Notifications, then check Settings → Reminders in the app.

Can I turn off the voice coach?
Yes: Settings → Workout → Voice coach. The coach speaks in your iPhone's language; for the best sound, download an enhanced voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?
Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
