Premium, subscriptions and payments

The core parts of Evde Fitness are free. Evde Fitness Premium unlocks the full plan, all levels and all quick workouts. Premium is offered in three ways: monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase. The current price and duration are shown in the app before you buy; prices may vary by country.

  • Payment: Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Apple processes all payments; your card or payment details never reach us or the app.
  • Free trial: The yearly plan may include a free trial (eligibility is determined by Apple). Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts as a paid subscription. Any unused portion of the trial is forfeited when you purchase another subscription during the trial.
  • Auto-renewal: The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. If the price changes, Apple informs you in advance.
  • Cancelling and managing: You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time on your iPhone in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or in the app via Settings > Manage subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; Premium remains available until then.
  • Refunds: Refund requests are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Your statutory rights are not affected.
  • Lifetime purchase: A one-time payment; it is not a subscription and does not renew. It is valid on the devices you use with the same Apple Account.
  • Restore: On a new device or after reinstalling the app, use Restore purchases to get your Premium access back.
